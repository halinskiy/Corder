• The menu-bar icon no longer intermittently clips to "( )". The idle ring was an SF Symbol whose rendered size sat at the menu-bar height limit, so macOS clipped its top and bottom (instead of scaling) whenever it re-laid-out the status item — on display sleep/wake, external-monitor connect/disconnect, or fullscreen transitions. It's now a fixed-size ring drawn the same way as the recording dot (which never clipped), and the button scales any icon down rather than clipping it.
