• Your voice no longer records sped-up/garbled when your mic switches to a different-rate device mid-recording (e.g. AirPods, which capture at 16 kHz, grabbing the mic a moment after recording started on the 44.1 kHz built-in mic). A 0.15.27 regression wrote the alignment padding in the new device's sample rate into the original file, scrambling the timeline. Padding now always matches the file, and a buffer that can't be resampled cleanly is dropped rather than written at the wrong rate.
  • Auto-title is always on now and no longer has a Settings toggle, every recording gets a generated title.
  • The bug-report button sends immediately instead of waiting out a 10-second countdown.
  • Sign-in copy now reads "unlocks unlimited sessions".
