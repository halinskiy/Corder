• Recordings take much less disk. Retained audio (your mic, the far end, and the playback mix) is now stored as 16 kHz mono 16-bit PCM — exactly what transcription and playback use — instead of the far larger 44.1/48 kHz stereo float32 the capture produced. Existing recordings are compacted in the background on launch. Combined with the removed silent backup track, a reported ~940 MB meeting drops to roughly ~110 MB with no change to transcript or playback quality.
